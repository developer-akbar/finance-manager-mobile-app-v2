# FinMan v2.2.1.3 — Changelog

---

## ✨ New Features

### Credit Card Account Type
- Added **Account Type** field to account settings with `Regular` and `💳 Credit Card` options.
- Credit card accounts store a **Settlement Date** (day of month the billing cycle closes, 1–28) and **Payment Due Days** (days after settlement date, 1–30).
- Input validation enforces valid ranges with clear error messages.
- Live preview of current billing cycle and next due date shown while editing.

### Credit Card Billing Cycle View (Accounts Screen)
- Credit card accounts display a **two-column row** — `Balance Payable` and `Outst. Balance` — instead of a single net balance.
- Column header labels appear above CC rows in every group containing a credit card account.
- Account group headers for all-CC groups show combined payable and outstanding totals.

### Credit Card Billing Cycle Logic
- **Balance Payable** = total charges from all closed billing cycles minus all payments made to date. Payments reduce this first.
- **Outstanding Balance** = net charges in the current open billing cycle. Overpayments spill over from payable and reduce this figure.
- When the card is overpaid beyond current cycle charges, Outstanding Balance shows a green `+` credit amount.

### CC Cycle Period in Account Drill-down
- Credit card accounts default to a new **CC Cycle** period tab in the drill-down view, scoped to the current billing cycle.
- CC Cycle is navigable with ‹ › arrows to browse past cycles. A **Current** badge marks the live cycle.
- A billing info strip shows the cycle's closing date.
- CC Cycle tab is hidden for non-CC accounts; all other period tabs remain available.

### Grouped Account List in Settings
- Settings → Accounts **List view** now organises accounts by Account Group in the same order as the Account Groups section.
- Each group renders a section header with an account count badge.
- Accounts within a group are indented for visual clarity.
- An **Ungrouped** section at the bottom collects accounts with no assigned group.
- Credit card accounts show a `💳 Credit Card · settles Nth` hint line inline without needing to open the edit panel.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Credit Card False Positives on Account Name
- Auto-detection of credit card type from account name is now limited to the word `credit` only. Previously `card` and `cc` were also matched, causing accounts like Food Card, Debit Card, and Prepaid Card to be incorrectly treated as credit cards.

### Account Type Not Persisting on Save
- Fixed a bug where setting an account to `Regular` did not persist after saving. The edit panel was overriding an explicitly saved empty `acctType` with a name-based detection fallback due to a falsy `||` check.

## 🏗 Notes
- DB schema updated: `accounts` table extended with `acct_type`, `settlement_date`, and `payment_due_days` columns. Existing Android (SQLite) databases migrate automatically via `ALTER TABLE` guards. Web (IndexedDB) version bumped to `7`.
- Payment due date is stored internally for future reminder features but not yet surfaced in the UI.
- Version: `2.2.1.3`
